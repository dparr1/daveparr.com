GIS Day 2026

Celebrate GIS Day at Davis Library

Wednesday, November 18 | 3:00 to 4:00 PM | Davis Library, 2nd floor, Room 247

Join University Libraries and the Department of Geography and Environment for UNC’s GIS Day Celebration and Map Competition. Look through a display of student maps, enjoy light snacks, and vote for your favorite map.

This year’s theme is Connecting NC: Transit, Active Transportation, and Safety. Winners are chosen half by the judges and half by popular vote, so your vote counts. Read more about the 2026 GIS Day Map Contest.

GEOG 115
Geospatial Technologies, Maps, and Society
MWF 9:05 to 9:55 AM | In person
Maps and geospatial technologies as both everyday technical tools and powerful ways of shaping knowledge. Explore how geographic information is collected, represented, and used to shape how we understand the world.
InstructorDr. Julia Cardwell
TopicsFundamentals of spatial data; GPS and remote sensing; cartographic design and map interpretation; history of mapping, power, and society
DatesJanuary 6 to May 6, 2027
Credits3.0
RegisterCRN 5414, Section 001. Registration is open now.

GEOG 586
Scaling Up Geospatial Analysis
MWF 2:30 to 3:20 PM
Ever wondered what happens when your GIS project gets too big for your laptop? This course takes the GIS skills you already have and applies them at larger scale: spatial databases, distributed computing, the cloud, and a university supercomputer, using common sources of big geospatial data.
InstructorDr. Julia Cardwell
ToolsDatabricks, SQL, DuckDB, Apache Spark
PrerequisiteGEOG 491 or equivalent
Email Dr. Cardwell

GEOG 592
GIS Programming with AI
MWF 2:30 to 3:20 PM
AI can write code. Can you make it solve a geographic problem? Combine AI-assisted programming with geographic knowledge, spatial analysis, and GIS software to automate tasks, analyze vector and raster data, build reusable tools, and develop a geospatial project.
InstructorDr. Jun Liang
You’ll usePython and ArcGIS Pro; AI-assisted development; R (sf and terra) and Google Earth Engine
PrerequisiteGEOG 370 or GEOG 491 (or equivalent GIS experience). No advanced programming background required.
