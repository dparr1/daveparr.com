GIS Day 2026

Celebrate GIS Day at Davis Library

Wednesday, November 18 | 3:00 to 4:00 PM | Davis Library, 2nd floor, Room 247

Join University Libraries and the Department of Geography and Environment for UNC’s GIS Day Celebration and Map Competition. Look through a display of student maps, enjoy light snacks, and vote for your favorite map.

This year’s theme is Connecting NC: Transit, Active Transportation, and Safety. Winners are chosen half by the judges and half by popular vote, so your vote counts. Read more about the 2026 GIS Day Map Contest.
