Over the summer of 2020, I served as a faculty research advisor for five MSU Denver students in the [Earth Data Science Corps](https://earthlab.colorado.edu/edsc) project managed and funded through [CU Boulder's Earth Lab](https://earthlab.colorado.edu/) project through an [NSF grant](https://www.nsf.gov/awardsearch/showAward?AWD_ID=1924337&HistoricalAwards=false).

Rese Donavan, Eddie Wolfe, Dominic Gardner, Jennifer Kerns, and Ken Doyle worked all summer learning Python, Data Science, and creating a project with me and Gabrielle Katz called *Plotting the Platte: Measuring Vernal Change Over Multiple Seasons Using LandSat Imagery*. You can see the final presentation here!
