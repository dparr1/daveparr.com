*What kinds of jobs does GIS lead to?*

GIS jobs include

* Climate Scientist
* App Developer
* Helicopter Pilot / Firefighter
* Conservationist
* GIS Analyst
* Health Geographer
* GIS Manager
* Civil Engineer
* Volcanologist
* Researcher
* Agricultural Analyst
* and Drone Pilot!
