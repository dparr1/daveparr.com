GEOG 491 builds on GEOG 370 with a methods-driven look at GIS. Students learn to design, carry out, and evaluate advanced spatial analyses in desktop GIS, build models of geographic processes, and plan and complete an independent project.

The prerequisite is GEOG 370, or permission of the instructor. The course is cross-listed as PLAN 491.
