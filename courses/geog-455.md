GEOG 455 is about communicating spatial information through maps and visualization. Students learn the cartographic and visualization concepts behind effective maps, make both static and interactive maps, and look at how design choices shape what readers see and understand.

The prerequisite is GEOG 370. The course counts toward the IDEAs in Action FC-CREATE requirement.
