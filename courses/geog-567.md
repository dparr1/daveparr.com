GEOG 567 teaches geospatial analysis in the cloud with Google Earth Engine. Students work with Earth Engine's catalog of satellite imagery and other geospatial data to monitor, map, model, and visualize environmental change at scales from local to global.

The prerequisite is GEOG 370, or permission of the instructor. The course counts toward the IDEAs in Action FC-NATSCI or FC-QUANT and RESEARCH requirements.
