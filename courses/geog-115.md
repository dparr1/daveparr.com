GEOG 115 is an introduction to maps and the technologies behind them, both as tools people use every day and as ways of knowing the world. It looks at how geographic information has been gathered, represented, and put to use over time, and at the role maps have played in building, reinforcing, and challenging power.

Along the way, students learn:

- **Spatial data basics:** what geographic data is and how places are described in it.
- **Geospatial technologies:** how GPS and remote sensing capture information about the Earth.
- **Cartography:** how maps are designed, and how to read and question them.
- **Maps and society:** how mapping has been used to make claims about places and people, and to push back against them.

There are no prerequisites.
