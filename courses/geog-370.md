GEOG 370 introduces geographic data and the core concepts of Geographic Information Science. Students learn how spatial data is built, managed, and represented, where it comes from and what its limits are, and how to work with it in QGIS and ArcGIS Pro. There are no prerequisites, so it suits students who are new to GIS as well as those who already have some experience. The course counts toward the IDEAs in Action FC-QUANT or FC-NATSCI requirement.

The course moves through three units, and each one ends with a project built on real-world data:

- **Data models and projections:** getting started with GIS, working with geographic information, and coordinate systems and map projections. The project is editing and querying spatial data.
- **Spatial data quality:** working with US Census data and georeferencing. The project is mapping Census data.
- **Spatial analysis:** density analysis, measurement, and weather data. The project is a GIS project of your own.

## Textbook

The course has its own free, interactive open textbook, written from the lectures. Chapters explain the concepts behind geographic information and show how to put them into practice in QGIS and ArcGIS Pro. New chapters are being added as they are written.
